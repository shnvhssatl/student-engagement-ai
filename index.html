How this version identifies seats

The teacher clicks each student's face centre during calibration. The browser stores the 40 seat-centre coordinates in this laptop's local storage. During monitoring, each detected face is matched to the nearest unused calibrated seat within the selected radius. If students change seats, recalibrate. This is seat-position mapping, not biometric identity recognition.

Lip movement is estimated visually from mouth landmarks. It reports “Possible Talking”; it does not record audio or prove that a student is speaking.
