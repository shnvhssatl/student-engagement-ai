Important limitation

Lip movement is estimated from MediaPipe mouth landmarks over time. The system reports “Possible Talking”; it cannot prove speech, identify the speaker by name, or reliably distinguish talking from chewing/yawning. With one front camera, far-away faces may be too small for reliable lip analysis. Performance depends on camera resolution, lighting and laptop speed.
